AUTO APPLICATOR: HOW TO INSTALL
===============================

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

1. In this window, drag "Auto Applicator" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.
   Then eject "Auto Applicator" in the Finder sidebar.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "Auto Applicator".

   The first time, your Mac may say it can't check the app for malicious software (it doesn't come from the
   App Store). To allow it, once:
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to the message about "Auto Applicator" and click "Open Anyway".
     - Enter your Mac password if asked, and click "Open Anyway" again.

3. Click "Start setup". In about five minutes the app walks you through everything in its own window:
     - add your resume (it reads it and fills in your details),
     - check your details and choose the jobs you want,
     - sign in to LinkedIn on LinkedIn's own page,
     - try a practice run (it fills in applications without sending them).

After that, open Auto Applicator whenever you want to apply, or let it run by itself each day (Settings).

YOUR DATA AND THE AI
Your details stay on your Mac. On macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on, the AI runs on your Mac with nothing
to download. Otherwise the app can download a free AI model (about 5 GB) the first time you ask it to.

TO REMOVE IT
Quit it, drag it from Applications to the Trash, and delete the folder
"Library/Application Support/AutoApplicator" in your home folder (in the Finder: Go menu > Go to Folder).
