AUTO APPLICATOR – HOW TO INSTALL
================================

You need: a Mac with macOS 13 or newer, 16 GB of memory, about 10 GB of free space, and an internet connection.

1. In this window, drag "Auto Applicator" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.
   (Then you can close this window and eject "Auto Applicator" in the Finder sidebar.)

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "Auto Applicator".

   The first time, your Mac says it can't check the app for malicious software (it doesn't come from the
   App Store). This only happens once. To allow it:
     - Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to the message about "Auto Applicator" and click "Open Anyway".
     - Enter your Mac password if asked, and click "Open Anyway" again.
   On macOS 13 or 14 you can instead right-click the app, choose "Open", then click "Open" again.

3. Click "Set up". Auto Applicator downloads what it needs (10–20 minutes the first time, in the background),
   and walks you through everything in its own window:
     - add your resume (it can read it and fill in your details for you),
     - check your details and choose the jobs you want,
     - sign in to LinkedIn,
     - try a practice run (it fills in applications without sending them).

After that, open Auto Applicator from your Applications folder (or the Dock) whenever you want to apply.

Your details stay on your Mac. The AI runs on your Mac too – nothing is sent to an online AI service.

To remove it: quit it, drag it from Applications to the Trash, and delete the folder
"Library/Application Support/AutoApplicator" in your home folder (in the Finder: Go menu > Go to Folder).
